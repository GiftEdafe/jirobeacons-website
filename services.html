Integrated professional capacity & technical execution.

Jiro-BEACONS provides clients with direct access to coordinated multidisciplinary AEC professional teams alongside specialized digital engineering workflows to support projects from early planning through handover.

WORKFORCE & ENGAGEMENT MODEL

Integrated AEC Professional Teams

Instead of recruiting and managing every professional independently, engage Jiro-BEACONS to supply and coordinate an integrated team under our professional management framework.

Project-Based Teams

A multidisciplinary team assembled and coordinated specifically for a defined project scope or delivery milestone.

Dedicated Professional Capacity

Selected engineers and specialists assigned to integrate seamlessly into your ongoing workload for an agreed duration.

Remote & Hybrid Delivery

Cross-border support for design coordination, quantity take-offs, and project controls, backed by local site personnel.

Core Professional Disciplines We Coordinate

We assemble expert teams tailored to your project requirements across the built environment:

Planning & Spatial Professionals

Town Planners, Land Surveyors & Geospatial Specialists: Executing land-use planning, site evaluations, high-precision boundary/topographic surveys, mapping, and development advisory.

Architecture & Engineering

Architects, Civil, Structural, Mechanical & Electrical Engineers: Delivering collaborative design coordination, structural calculations, building services integration, and technical reviews.

Cost & Construction Management

Quantity Surveyors, Builders, Planners & Project Managers: Driving cost estimation, bills of quantities (BOQs), construction programming, site monitoring, and commercial contract controls.

Quality, Compliance & Information

QA/QC Inspectors, Quality Engineers & Document Controllers: Managing rigorous inspection and test records, material compliance evidence, technical registers, and structured documentation.

TECHNICAL DELIVERABLES

Complete Digital Engineering Package

Beyond team deployment, our specialized Digital Engineering Package (DEP) brings together the critical technical data generated during project execution into a single unified deliverable.

Digital Engineering Package (DEP)

Engineered for projects requiring complete traceability from site to handover, DEP connects quality evidence, technical documentation, as-builts, and reality capture into a structured digital asset.

Digital QA/QC Oversight Quality evidence, inspection logs and compliance traceability.
Engineering Document Control Structured technical registers and central project archives.
Digital Test Packs Organized inspection, testing and commissioning verification logs.
Digital As-Built Index Systematic indexing of final drawings, certifications and records.
Survey & Geospatial Records High-accuracy spatial datasets supporting verification.
Scan-to-BIM Point-cloud processing and intelligent 3D asset modeling.
