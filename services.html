SIGNATURE DIGITAL DELIVERY

Our Signature Digital Engineering Package

Jiro-BEACONS’ signature Digital Engineering Package structures project quality records, technical documentation, as-built information and agreed digital deliverables into a coordinated handover package.

Digital Engineering Package (DEP)

Developed progressively during project execution, DEP helps project teams organise engineering evidence and prepare asset information for downstream use, aligned with the agreed deliverables.

Digital QA/QC Oversight Quality evidence, inspection logs and compliance traceability.
Engineering Document Control Structured technical registers and central project archives.
Digital Test Packs Organized inspection, testing and commissioning verification logs.
Digital As-Built Index Systematic indexing of final drawings, certifications and records.
Survey & Geospatial Records High-accuracy spatial datasets supporting verification.
Scan-to-BIM & Digital Twins Point-cloud processing and intelligent 3D asset modeling, where scope is defined.
