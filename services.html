OUR SERVICES

Engineering information from project delivery to asset lifecycle.

Jiro-BEACONS combines integrated AEC professional teams, engineering quality, documentation, geospatial and digital workflows to help project teams create information that remains useful beyond construction.

WORKFORCE & DELIVERY MODEL

Integrated AEC Professional Teams

Access coordinated multidisciplinary Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) professionals through a single project-focused engagement. Instead of recruiting and coordinating every professional independently, engage Jiro-BEACONS to assemble and manage the right team for your defined scope.

Project-Based Teams

A coordinated multidisciplinary team assembled for a specific project, set of deliverables, or milestone execution phase.

Dedicated Professional Capacity

Selected engineering and specialist professionals assigned to support ongoing client workloads for an agreed duration.

Remote & Hybrid Delivery

Cross-border support for design coordination, BIM, quantity take-offs, and project controls with local site personnel as required.
