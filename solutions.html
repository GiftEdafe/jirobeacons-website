OUR SOLUTIONS

Engineering information around real project needs.

Different projects generate different information challenges. Jiro-BEACONS combines engineering documentation, Digital QA/QC, geospatial data and digital asset workflows around the needs of project teams and asset owners.

PROJECT ENVIRONMENTS

Solutions for the information lifecycle.

Whether the requirement is project control, quality evidence, digital handover, existing-condition information or asset management, our workflows are designed to make engineering information easier to trace and use.

01

Construction & EPC

Organize inspection records, material documentation, testing evidence, technical documents and progress information throughout project execution.

The objective is a more traceable information trail from construction activities through final handover.

02

Energy & Infrastructure

Support complex infrastructure projects with structured engineering records, QA/QC evidence, as-built information and digital handover workflows.

Particularly suited to projects where asset information must remain useful after construction.

03

Real Estate & Property

Create organized building information that helps property owners understand what was constructed, installed and documented.

This can support future maintenance, refurbishment, compliance and operational planning.

04

Building Retrofit

Capture existing building and MEP information to support retrofit planning where accurate existing-condition information is required.

Scan-to-BIM and digital asset information can help establish a structured view of existing conditions.

05

Asset Owners & Operators

Organize engineering information into a more accessible digital asset record that can support operations, maintenance and future modifications.

06

Project Handover

Move beyond simply transferring folders of documents. Structure the evidence, records and as-built information required for a usable digital handover.
