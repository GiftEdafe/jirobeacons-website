01 / Project Delivery

Construction & EPC Project Delivery

For contractors, project managers, EPC teams and developers facing complex multi-stakeholder execution environments.

The Challenge

Engineering disciplines, procurement records, site activities, inspections and project documentation can become fragmented across teams.

Our Solution

Integrate multidisciplinary AEC expertise, construction coordination, QA/QC oversight and engineering information management within the agreed delivery scope.

Value Delivered

Better coordination, more traceable project evidence and a clearer route from construction activities to project close-out and handover.

Related Services Engineering & Project Coordination · Construction Delivery Support · QA/QC, Inspection & Testing
02 / Quality & Governance

Digital QA/QC & Project Assurance

For quality managers, contractors, consultants and project owners seeking verifiable quality standards.

The Challenge

Inspection results, material certificates, test reports, non-conformance records and approvals may be scattered, difficult to retrieve or disconnected from the relevant work activities.

Our Solution

Establish structured digital QA/QC workflows and records, with traceability between work activities, inspections, test evidence, approvals and project documentation.

Value Delivered

More accessible quality evidence, improved record traceability and better-prepared project handover documentation.

Related Services QA/QC, Inspection & Testing · Engineering Information Management
03 / Reality Capture & BIM

Digital As-Built, Scan-to-BIM & Asset Information

For property owners, facility managers, retrofit teams and infrastructure operators managing existing assets.

The Challenge

Existing buildings and infrastructure may have incomplete drawings, outdated layouts or insufficient information about installed assets and building systems.

Our Solution

Combine surveying, reality capture where appropriate, as-built documentation and structured asset information. For suitable projects, Scan-to-BIM can support the creation of 3D representations of existing conditions, including relevant architectural and MEP systems.

Value Delivered

A more reliable view of existing conditions to support retrofit planning, maintenance coordination, asset identification and future project decisions.

Related Services Surveying & Geospatial · Engineering Information Management
04 / Property & Assets

Real Estate & Property Information

For developers, estate managers, property owners and investors requiring streamlined asset governance.

The Challenge

Property, survey, construction-quality and asset records may be dispersed across paper files, spreadsheets and separate project teams.

Our Solution

Organise available survey records, as-built information, construction evidence and relevant property or asset documentation into a structured, retrievable information system, subject to the client's requirements and data permissions.

Value Delivered

Better access to property information, improved records continuity and a stronger evidence base for property management, transactions and future improvements.

Related Services Surveying & Geospatial · Quantity Surveying & Cost Support · Engineering Information Management
05 / Critical Infrastructure

Energy & Critical Infrastructure

For energy developers, industrial operators, contractors and asset owners managing complex industrial systems.

The Challenge

Complex facilities require coordinated technical records, construction-quality evidence, inspection documentation and dependable handover information.

Our Solution

Apply relevant engineering coordination, surveying, QA/QC and digital information management capabilities to projects across power, energy, oil and gas, industrial facilities and energy-transition infrastructure, including battery energy storage systems (BESS), where applicable.

Value Delivered

Better organised project evidence, clearer information traceability and structured information to support asset handover and subsequent operations.

Related Services Core Infrastructure Engineering · QA/QC, Inspection & Testing · Engineering Information Management
06 / Intelligent Operations

Smart Buildings, Carbon & ESG Intelligence

For building owners, facility operators, developers and sustainability teams aiming for high-performance operations.

The Challenge

Building energy use, operating conditions and sustainability information may not be sufficiently connected to support timely decisions or reliable performance reporting.

Our Solution

Through Smart Building Telemetry & Integration and the planned EdgeCarbon Compliance & ESG Data Platforms, the company aims to connect relevant building data, monitoring hardware and analytics. The proposed capabilities include Real-Time Hardware Nodes, Carbon Tax Shield, Peak Demand Alerters and Anti-Greenwashing Ledger.

Value Delivered

A pathway toward improved operational visibility, energy-demand awareness and more traceable sustainability data, subject to the deployed systems, data quality and applicable reporting requirements.

Related Services Smart Building Telemetry & Integration · EdgeCarbon Compliance & ESG Data Platforms
