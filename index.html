Engineering Across the Asset Lifecycle

From understanding an existing property or site to planning, design, construction, verification, digital delivery and future operations, Jiro-BEACONS provides integrated engineering support across the lifecycle of physical assets.

Our Integrated Engineering Lifecycle

We connect multidisciplinary engineering activities so that information generated at one stage can continue to support decisions at the next.

01. Survey & Evaluate

Surveying, geospatial information, property assessment and existing-asset evaluation.

02. Quantity & Cost

Measurement, quantity surveying, cost information and commercial project support.

03. Plan

Engineering planning, scope definition and project delivery requirements.

04. Design

Civil, structural and building engineering design coordination and technical development.

05. Build

Construction and engineering delivery support across building and infrastructure projects.

06. Verify & Assure

QA/QC, inspection, testing, NDT and construction quality verification.

07. Digitalize

Digital engineering, BIM, Scan-to-BIM, as-built and structured asset information.

08. Handover & Operate

Digital handover, asset information and lifecycle support for future operations.
