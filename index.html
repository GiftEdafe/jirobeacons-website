Our Guiding Principles

Our Vision

Our Mission

To deliver uncompromising, independent infrastructure quality assurance, advanced spatial data integration, and smart building solutions that empower better engineering and business decisions.

Our Values

  • Integrity & Independence: Providing objective quality assurance and rigorous compliance oversight.
  • Collaboration & Inclusion: Fostering multidisciplinary teamwork and diverse perspectives to drive innovation.
  • Excellence & Precision: Maintaining exacting standards across surveying, digital twins, and project execution.

Our History & Growth

Jiro-BEACONS Root: President and Principal Consultant Gift Edafe began her professional journey in QA/QC data entry in 2011, advancing through roles as a surveyor technician, construction laboratory assistant, civil structural inspector, and QA/QC Lead Auditor before founding Jiro-BEACONS Engineering in 2018 with one foundational vision and bold ambitions. From our early roots in infrastructure quality assurance, we expanded into spatial data integration, smart building telemetry, and digital twins, redefining how technology drives the future of construction.

How We Are Expanding: Scaling our operations across international markets, incorporating advanced BIM frameworks, and participating in incubator programs like Start GBC in Ontario to commercialize smart building innovations.

Building Careers: Cultivating top-tier talent in civil engineering, geospatial surveying, and digital technology while building collaborative co-founder and project leadership networks.

Workers Welfare: Cultivating an inclusive, safe, and supportive organizational culture that prioritizes professional growth, equity, and the wellbeing of every team member.

Redefining Construction: Pioneering how Smart Building Design & Operations shape the new age of construction through IoT telemetry, edge-carbon tracking, and digital twin lifecycle management.
