Corporate Capabilities & Philosophy

Who We Are

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is an integrated engineering and construction delivery consultancy that brings together multidisciplinary AEC professionals and digital engineering capabilities to support infrastructure throughout its lifecycle. We unite multidisciplinary engineering and construction expertise with digital delivery to create infrastructure supported by reliable project information and lasting asset intelligence.

Our Multidisciplinary AEC Teams

Our team model integrates architecture, engineering, surveying, quantity surveying, construction, quality assurance, and project information specialists, assembled dynamically according to specific project requirements.

Engineering, Construction & EPCIC Delivery

We approach planning, technical coordination, procurement, construction, installation, and commissioning rigorously according to agreed project scopes and delivery arrangements. Full EPCIC execution is claimed where contractual scope, qualified teams, procurement arrangements, and installation responsibilities fully support the claim; otherwise, structured coordination and support applies.

Our Signature Digital Engineering Package (DEP)

Digital QA/QC records, engineering documentation, as-built information, and asset data are structured into a reliable digital handover. These records are developed progressively throughout delivery, ensuring a continuous digital legacy rather than a retrospective assembly.

Our Commitment

We uphold uncompromised principles: engineering excellence, coordinated delivery, traceable quality, information integrity, compliance, and lifecycle value.

Our Vision

To advance a more intelligent, digitally connected approach to infrastructure delivery and asset operations globally, bridging physical execution with intelligent digital data ecosystems.

Our Sectors, Partnerships & Project Experience

We serve real estate, civil infrastructure, energy, oil & gas, industrial facilities, and smart building sectors through collaborative professional networks and demonstrated project evidence.

Jiro-BEACONS Root

President and Principal Consultant Gift Edafe began her professional journey in QA/QC data entry in 2011, advancing through roles as a surveyor technician, construction laboratory assistant, civil structural inspector, and QA/QC Lead Auditor before founding Jiro-BEACONS Engineering in 2018 with one foundational vision and bold ambitions. From our early roots in infrastructure quality assurance, we expanded into spatial data integration, smart building telemetry, and digital twins, redefining how technology drives the future of construction.
