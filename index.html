Who We Are

More than documentation.
Integrated engineering.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering is an integrated engineering and digital infrastructure company supporting the full lifecycle of physical assets — from survey and evaluation through planning, design, construction, verification, digitalization and handover.

Our work connects what is physically built with the structured information required to control, verify, manage and operate it.

We don't simply record project information. We engineer the information behind the infrastructure.
