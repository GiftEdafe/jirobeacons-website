Who We Are

More than documentation.
Integrated engineering.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering is an integrated engineering and digital infrastructure company supporting the lifecycle of physical assets — from survey and evaluation through quantity and cost support, planning, design coordination, construction, verification, digitalization and handover.

We connect field activities and engineering requirements with structured information that helps project teams improve visibility, maintain traceability and make better-informed decisions.
