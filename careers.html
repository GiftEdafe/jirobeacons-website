Our Network Model

A Network of Engineering Expertise

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is developing an integrated engineering and intelligent project-delivery business that connects professional expertise, construction quality, engineering information and digital asset solutions.

Our approach combines conventional engineering and project support with structured digital information to help clients improve project coordination, quality assurance, documentation, handover and asset visibility. We also develop coordinated professional-team solutions, connecting clients with suitable AEC specialists according to project requirements, professional competence and applicable regulatory requirements.

Please note that our professional network is actively being developed to scale across upcoming project requirements, rather than implying that every specialist role is currently a full-time funded staff vacancy.

Active Recruitment Remote · Global · Commission-Based

Global Business Development Partner

Engineering | Construction | Integrated AEC Professional Teams | Digital Engineering

Company: Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited
Location: Remote — open to qualified candidates globally
Engagement Type: Independent, performance-based commercial partnership
Compensation: Commission-based; no guaranteed base salary at initial stage
Market Focus: Nigeria initially, with opportunities to develop international markets
Reports To: Founder & CEO

About the Role

We are seeking a commercially resourceful Business Development Partner to help translate our technical capabilities into client relationships, qualified opportunities, strategic partnerships and sustainable revenue. This opportunity is intended for an experienced B2B business development professional who understands consultative selling, can engage business decision-makers and is motivated by building commercial opportunities.

You will work directly with the Founder to identify prospective clients, understand their requirements, develop a qualified sales pipeline, coordinate proposals and progress suitable opportunities towards contract award.

What You Will Help Us Sell

  • Integrated AEC Professional Teams: Coordinated access to architecture, town planning, land surveying, civil/structural engineering, quantity surveying, building, QA/QC, project management, and document control expertise.
  • Engineering, Construction & QA/QC: Project support, quality assurance and control coordination, inspection and testing, digital QA/QC oversight, and construction documentation workflows.
  • Digital Engineering & Asset Information: Scan-to-BIM, existing-asset capture, digital engineering packages, structured digital records, and digital-twin lifecycle data support.
  • Quantity, Cost & Project Information Services: Quantity surveying, cost documentation, measurement support, and records management workflows.

Key Responsibilities

  • Business Development & Expansion: Identify target clients, sectors, and commercially viable opportunities across contractors, developers, engineering consultancies, and asset owners.
  • Lead Generation & Conversion: Initiate professional contact, arrange discovery meetings, qualify opportunities, coordinate proposals with the Founder, and progress negotiations towards contract award.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Identify complementary consultancies, explore subcontracting or joint pursuits, and develop transparent referral relationships.
  • Commercial Reporting: Maintain an organized sales pipeline, report risks/feedback regularly, and track agreed performance indicators.

Required Experience & Capabilities

  • Experience in B2B business development, consultative sales, commercial partnerships, or business acquisition.
  • Strong professional communication, negotiation, presentation, and relationship-management skills.
  • Self-management, reliability, and the ability to work independently in a remote arrangement across time zones.
  • Confidence engaging senior decision-makers and coordinating with technical professionals.
  • Demonstrated commercial capability and ability to explain previous results without disclosing confidential data.

Compensation & Commercial Terms

This is an initial commission-based independent business development partnership, not a salaried employment position. There is no guaranteed base salary at the initial stage. Commission rates, payment terms, eligible transactions, and confidentiality agreements will be discussed and agreed in writing before the engagement begins.
