Active Recruitment Remote · Global · Commission-Based

Global Business Development Partner

Engineering | Construction | Integrated AEC Professional Teams | Digital Engineering

Company: Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited
Location: Remote — open to qualified candidates globally
Engagement Type: Independent, performance-based commercial partnership
Compensation: Commission-based; no guaranteed base salary at initial stage
Market Focus: Nigeria initially, with opportunities to develop international markets
Reports To: Director

About the Role

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is developing an integrated engineering and intelligent project-delivery business that connects professional expertise, construction quality, engineering information and digital asset solutions.

Our approach combines conventional engineering and project support with structured digital information to help clients improve project coordination, quality assurance, documentation, handover and asset visibility. We also develop coordinated professional-team solutions, connecting clients with suitable AEC specialists according to project requirements, professional competence and applicable regulatory requirements.

We are seeking a commercially resourceful Business Development Partner to help translate these capabilities into client relationships, qualified opportunities, strategic partnerships and sustainable revenue. This opportunity is intended for an experienced B2B business development professional who understands consultative selling, can engage business decision-makers and is motivated by building commercial opportunities.

You will work directly with the Director to identify prospective clients, understand their requirements, develop a qualified sales pipeline, coordinate proposals and progress suitable opportunities towards contract award. The role is broader than social media promotion or lead-list preparation. We are looking for someone who can take ownership of the business development process and contribute measurable commercial value.

What You Will Help Us Sell

  • 1. Integrated AEC Professional Teams: Coordinated access to architecture, town planning, land surveying, civil and structural engineering, quantity surveying, building, QA/QC, project management and document control expertise. Potential engagement models include project-based teams, dedicated capacity, remote/hybrid technical support, and specialist assignments.
  • 2. Engineering, Construction & QA/QC: Engineering and construction project support, quality assurance and quality control coordination, inspection and testing coordination, digital QA/QC oversight, construction documentation, and digital handover support.
  • 3. Digital Engineering & Asset Information: Scan-to-BIM and existing-asset information capture, Digital Engineering Packages, as-built information, asset registers, and digital-twin-related lifecycle data support.
  • 4. Quantity, Cost & Project Information Services: Quantity surveying, cost information, measurement support, engineering/construction records management, and structured information workflows.

Key Responsibilities

  • Business Development & Market Expansion: Identify target clients, sectors, and commercially viable opportunities across contractors, EPC companies, developers, engineering consultancies, infrastructure organizations, energy companies, and asset owners. Research accounts, decision-makers, and upcoming projects.
  • Lead Generation & Sales Conversion: Initiate professional contact, arrange discovery meetings, qualify opportunities based on need, budget, and timing, coordinate proposals with the Director, and progress negotiations towards contract award while keeping accurate records.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Identify complementary consultancies and service providers, explore subcontracting or joint pursuits, and develop transparent referral relationships.
  • Commercial Reporting: Maintain an organized sales pipeline, provide regular updates on prospect engagement, report commercial risks and competitor activity, and track agreed performance indicators.

Required Experience & Capabilities

  • Experience in B2B business development, consultative sales, commercial partnerships, or business acquisition.
  • Ability to identify prospects, initiate conversations, qualify opportunities, and progress sales.
  • Strong professional communication, negotiation, presentation, and relationship-management skills.
  • Commercial judgement and an understanding of how business opportunities become profitable contracts.
  • Self-management, reliability, and the ability to work independently in a remote arrangement.
  • Confidence engaging senior decision-makers and coordinating with technical professionals.

Compensation & Commercial Terms

This is an initial commission-based independent business development partnership, not a salaried employment position. There is no guaranteed base salary at the initial stage. Commission rates and payment terms will be discussed and agreed in writing before the engagement begins. The written agreement will define eligible transactions, commission calculation, timing, repeat business, and confidentiality terms.
