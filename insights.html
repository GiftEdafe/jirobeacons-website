Featured Insight · Project Quality & QA/QC

A Construction Project Can Pass Inspection and Still Have a Documentation Problem

Physical inspection confirms aspects of the work observed at a particular stage. Reliable inspection records, test results, material traceability and organised handover information help establish what was completed, what was verified and what evidence remains available.

The Challenge of Fragmented Records

During active construction, site teams focus heavily on achieving physical milestones. However, test reports from independent laboratories, material mill certificates, and non-conformance sign-offs often reside in disparate silos—some on paper folders in site trailers, others in local desktop spreadsheets or individual email chains.

The Impact on Handover and Closeout

When project handover arrives, verifying that every completed element meets specification becomes an administrative bottleneck if the evidentiary trail is incomplete. Asset owners inherit facilities with obscured test history, making future maintenance or warranty claims unnecessarily cumbersome.

The Jiro-BEACONS Approach

By establishing structured digital inspection logs and rigorous document control protocols during execution, project teams ensure that physical completion is matched by transparent, instantly retrievable compliance records.

The Cost of Information Latency

In modern engineering projects, time spent searching for the latest revision of a structural drawing or a specific material test certificate accumulates into thousands of lost man-hours annually. When inquiries take days rather than seconds, project momentum stalls.

Cloud-Enabled Common Data Environments (CDE)

Implementing structured SharePoint and cloud collaboration architectures ensures single-source-of-truth reliability. Granular permission management combined with standardized naming conventions enables teams to locate authorized documentation instantly.

Surveying & Existing Assets

Why Every Existing Building Needs Reliable As-Built Information Before Major Renovation

The critical role of precise site measurements, Scan-to-BIM, and baseline spatial data in avoiding costly retrofit surprises.

Navigating Legacy Discrepancies

Renovating older structures based on outdated historical drawings frequently results in unforeseen site clashes during construction, leading to costly variations and delays.

The Power of Reality Capture

Mobile LiDAR and high-precision laser scanning capture exact spatial geometry of existing conditions, translating physical spaces into intelligent point clouds and coordinated BIM models that mitigate retrofit risk.
