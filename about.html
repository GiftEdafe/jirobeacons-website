Who We Are

An integrated approach to engineering and project information.

Better project outcomes begin with reliable evidence, coordinated delivery and information that remains useful beyond construction.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited brings together engineering, surveying, construction quality and digital information practices to support the delivery and management of physical assets.

Our approach connects surveying and evaluation, quantity and cost considerations, engineering coordination, construction assurance, project documentation and digital asset information. The aim is to make project evidence more structured, traceable and accessible to the people who need it.

From existing-building information to construction quality records and digital handover packages, we focus on creating a clearer relationship between what is built, what is verified and what is documented.

Our Philosophy

We don't just record progress. We engineer the information behind it.

Engineering information should do more than fill folders. It should help teams understand what happened, establish what has been verified, trace decisions and make informed choices.

We therefore value evidence, consistency, clear ownership and information structures that support real project requirements. Digital tools matter most when they make engineering information more useful, not simply more complicated.

Looking Ahead

Connecting engineering information with smarter asset decisions.

As infrastructure and buildings become more data-connected, structured engineering information can provide a foundation for richer asset visibility, more informed operational decisions and improved reporting.

Our strategic direction includes the development and integration of digital workflows that connect existing-asset information, building-system data and environmental performance information where the project requirements, technology and validated data support them.

Areas of strategic development

  • Digital twins and structured asset information for existing buildings and infrastructure.
  • Smart-building telemetry and integration, including project-specific connections to building-system data.
  • Carbon information, ESG data and more structured compliance-reporting workflows.
  • AI-enabled engineering workflows that help organise, analyse and retrieve project information responsibly.

Implementation, integrations and reporting outputs depend on the asset, available data, validation requirements, technology and agreed project scope.
