Corporate Overview

Integrated Engineering. Construction Delivery. Intelligent Infrastructure.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited delivers integrated engineering and construction solutions across infrastructure, buildings, energy, and industrial projects. Through multidisciplinary AEC professional teams, coordinated project delivery, and our signature Digital Engineering Package (DEP), we connect planning, engineering, procurement, construction, quality assurance, and commissioning with intelligent digital capabilities.

Our approach combines physical project execution with rigorous engineering controls and structured asset information—supporting reliable delivery today and smarter infrastructure operations for the future.

03 / Signature Deliverable

Our Signature Digital Engineering Package (DEP)

The Digital Engineering Package (DEP) is Jiro-BEACONS' signature digital delivery framework, integrating QA/QC records, engineering documentation, as-built information, and asset data progressively throughout the project lifecycle to support coordinated delivery and structured digital handover.

Rather than being assembled retrospectively at project closeout, DEP information is developed incrementally through planning, design, and construction, in line with the agreed project scope and information requirements.

  • Progressive QA/QC test packs and inspection records
  • Controlled engineering document registers
  • Digital as-built indexes and geospatial data
04 / Quality & Governance

Digital QA/QC & Intelligent Asset Information

Infrastructure reliability depends on verifiable evidence. Our digital QA/QC approach brings together field inspection records, material testing results, and structured quality data to strengthen traceability, support technical verification, and reduce information gaps throughout project delivery.

These records can be organised into structured digital asset registers, helping facility managers and asset owners access reliable asset information to support maintenance planning, operational decisions, and long-term infrastructure performance.

  • Digital QA/QC Records & Inspection Traceability
  • Material Testing & Technical Verification Records
  • Structured Digital Asset Registers
  • As-Built Information & Handover Data

Foundational Heritage

The Jiro-BEACONS Journey

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering was founded by Gift Edafe in 2018 to provide the rigorous engineering, disciplined quality management, precise measurement, and reliable project intelligence that infrastructure clients need to deliver successfully.

Gift began his career in 2011 in QA/QC data entry, developing an early appreciation for the importance of accurate information in project delivery. He later gained experience in surveying, construction laboratory support, civil and structural inspection, and QA/QC leadership. This broad expertise revealed a persistent industry challenge: engineering decisions, construction quality, and project data are often managed in isolation.

Jiro-BEACONS was established to bridge this gap by combining practical engineering expertise with digital capability. The company integrates the information, processes, and technologies that help clients make informed decisions throughout the infrastructure lifecycle.

Today, Jiro-BEACONS delivers an integrated portfolio spanning engineering and construction, geospatial intelligence, digital QA/QC, smart building technologies, and digital twin solutions. By connecting physical assets with trusted, actionable data, the company helps clients improve delivery confidence, strengthen quality assurance, optimize asset management, and sustain performance over time.
