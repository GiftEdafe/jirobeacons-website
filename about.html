01 / Corporate Identity

Who We Are & Our Multidisciplinary AEC Teams

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering is an integrated engineering and construction delivery consultancy. We bring together multidisciplinary architecture, engineering, surveying, quantity surveying, construction, quality assurance, and project information specialists.

Rather than working in isolated silos, our professionals assemble dynamically according to specific project requirements, ensuring that physical construction and engineering controls operate as one cohesive unit.

  • Integrated AEC Professional Teams
  • Seamless cross-discipline coordination
  • Independent and objective quality assurance
02 / Execution Model

Engineering, Construction & EPCIC Delivery

We approach planning, technical coordination, procurement, construction, installation, and commissioning rigorously according to agreed project scopes and delivery arrangements.

Commercial Distinction: Full EPCIC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction, Installation, and Commissioning) execution is claimed only where our contractual scope, qualified team, procurement arrangements, and installation responsibilities fully support the claim. For other engagements, structured project coordination and execution support is provided.

03 / Signature Deliverable

Our Signature Digital Engineering Package (DEP)

The DEP is our continuous digital delivery process. Digital QA/QC records, engineering documentation, as-built information, and asset data are structured progressively throughout delivery into a reliable digital handover.

Crucially, DEP records are built incrementally during planning, design, and construction, rather than being assembled retrospectively at the end of a project.

  • Progressive QA/QC test packs & inspection records
  • Controlled engineering document registers
  • Digital as-built indexes and geospatial data
04 / Quality & Governance

Digital QA/QC & Intelligent Asset Information

Infrastructure reliability depends on verifiable evidence. Our digital quality control systems combine rigorous field inspections, material testing records, and transparent data logging to eliminate ambiguity.

This information flows directly into intelligent asset registers, empowering facility managers and asset owners with actionable operational clarity from day one.

Foundational Heritage

Jiro-BEACONS Root

President and Principal Consultant Gift Edafe began her professional journey in QA/QC data entry in 2011, advancing through roles as a surveyor technician, construction laboratory assistant, civil structural inspector, and QA/QC Lead Auditor before founding Jiro-BEACONS Engineering in 2018 with one foundational vision and bold ambitions. From our early roots in infrastructure quality assurance, we expanded into spatial data integration, smart building telemetry, and digital twins, redefining how technology drives the future of construction.
