About Jiro-BEACONS

Engineering with a wider view.

We bring together engineering disciplines, physical project delivery and digital information to create better outcomes across the infrastructure lifecycle.

Who We Are

An integrated engineering company built around the asset.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is an integrated engineering and digital infrastructure company serving projects, property and asset-intensive businesses.

Our capability spans surveying and evaluation, quantity surveying, engineering planning and smart building design, construction, quality assurance and quality control, digital engineering, asset information and project handover.

This multidisciplinary structure allows us to look beyond individual project activities and understand how decisions, physical work, quality evidence and information connect across the lifecycle of an asset.

The result is a more connected approach to engineering — from understanding what exists, to delivering what is planned, verifying what has been built and structuring the information needed for what comes next.

How We Think

Engineering is more than a collection of disciplines.

Our approach is built around the relationship between infrastructure, information, people and decisions.

01

Think Lifecycle

We consider how an asset will be surveyed, planned, built, verified, documented, handed over and ultimately operated — not just what happens during one project phase.

02

Connect Disciplines

Surveying, quantity surveying, engineering, construction, QA/QC and digital engineering become more valuable when their information can work together.

03

Engineer the Evidence

Good project decisions require reliable evidence. We focus on traceability, structured records and information that can be understood and trusted later.

04

Build for What Comes Next

Project information should remain useful after construction. We structure information with future handover, maintenance, improvement and asset decisions in mind.

05

Use Technology with Purpose

Digital tools are valuable when they improve engineering visibility, coordination, verification and decision-making — not simply because they are new.

06

Protect Information

Project and asset information can be commercially sensitive. We approach documentation and digital information with confidentiality, control and responsible use in mind.

Our Capability

One engineering perspective across multiple disciplines.

Modern infrastructure projects rarely fit neatly into one discipline. Survey information influences design. Quantities influence cost. Construction creates quality evidence. Verification creates confidence. Digital information connects it all.

Jiro-BEACONS is structured to operate across these interfaces, bringing the right technical capability together around the needs of the project or asset.

  • Surveying, Geospatial & Existing-Asset Evaluation
  • Quantity Surveying & Cost Support
  • Engineering Planning & Design
  • Construction & Project Delivery
  • QA/QC, Inspection & Testing
  • Digital Engineering & Scan-to-BIM
  • Digital Asset Information & Handover

Our Difference

We see the physical asset and the information about that asset as connected engineering problems.

That perspective allows us to help clients move from fragmented project records and disconnected technical activities toward information that can support real engineering and business decisions.

Professional Leadership

Jiro-BEACONS was founded with a practical understanding of engineering delivery, surveying, quality control, the growing importance of reliable project information and with a clear belief that infrastructure projects can be managed better when their information is treated as an engineering asset.

The company's direction is therefore not limited to conventional project documentation. It is focused on building an integrated engineering organization capable of supporting physical infrastructure while developing the digital information systems required to understand, verify and operate those assets.

Our Vision

To help build a world where infrastructure is understood as clearly in data as it is in the physical world.

We are building toward a globally connected engineering organization capable of combining technical expertise, distributed talent and digital infrastructure to serve projects and businesses across markets.
