Who we are

An integrated engineering approach to physical and digital projects.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is an engineering and project information company focused on connecting physical infrastructure with the technical information behind it.

Our approach brings together surveying and geospatial information, quantity and cost support, engineering coordination, construction quality assurance, project documentation and digital asset information.

Rather than treating these activities as isolated tasks, we look at how information moves through the project lifecycle—from early measurements and planning to construction verification, handover and future asset use.

The scope of each engagement is tailored to the project and delivered through the relevant professional expertise, specialist resources and agreed responsibilities.

Our philosophy

We don't just record progress. We engineer the information behind it.

Infrastructure produces a substantial amount of information: survey data, drawings, inspection reports, test results, construction records, equipment schedules and as-built details.

When these records are incomplete, disconnected or difficult to retrieve, project teams can struggle to verify decisions, demonstrate quality and prepare for handover.

We focus on making project information more structured, traceable and useful—so that it can support real decisions during delivery and throughout the asset lifecycle.

  • Connect field activities with reliable project records.
  • Make quality evidence traceable to inspections and test results.
  • Organize digital as-built and asset information for future use.
  • Support better coordination between engineering disciplines.

What guides us

Built around evidence, coordination and long-term usefulness.

Our working principles shape how we organize information, approach project requirements and collaborate with clients and project teams.

01

Evidence before assumption

We value traceable project records, identifiable sources and documented results. Information should support verification rather than simply fill a register.

02

Coordination across disciplines

We recognize that project outcomes depend on the connection between technical disciplines, site activities, commercial considerations and information requirements.

03

Information designed for use

We structure records with retrieval, accountability, handover and future reference in mind—not only the immediate reporting requirement.

04

Practical digital adoption

We focus on digital workflows that address defined project and asset needs, with appropriate attention to data quality and implementation requirements.

05

Clear scope and responsibility

Professional responsibilities, deliverables and interfaces should be defined clearly for each engagement and aligned with applicable requirements.

06

Lifecycle perspective

We consider how information created during surveying, design and construction may support handover, maintenance and future asset decisions.
