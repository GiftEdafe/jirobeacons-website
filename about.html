ABOUT JIRO-BEACONS

Engineering information for smarter infrastructure.

We connect engineering delivery, quality evidence, documentation, geospatial information and digital asset data into structured information that remains useful beyond project completion.

Who We Are

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited is an engineering consultancy focused on integrated engineering delivery, Digital QA/QC, engineering documentation, geospatial information and digital asset delivery.

Our approach recognizes that modern infrastructure projects generate significant amounts of technical information during design, construction, inspection, testing and handover.

We help project teams structure that information so it can be traced, accessed and used rather than becoming disconnected records after construction.

Our Philosophy

We don't just build infrastructure. We engineer the data.

The physical asset is only part of the project outcome. The information created around that asset can support compliance, handover, maintenance, operations and future decision-making.

Our Approach

We combine engineering knowledge, quality management, documentation discipline and spatial information to create practical digital workflows for project and asset teams.

This includes Digital QA/QC oversight, structured engineering documentation, digital test packs, as-built information, geospatial records, Scan-to-BIM workflows and digital asset information.

LEADERSHIP

Founder & CEO

Gift Edafe is the Founder & CEO of Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited, with professional experience spanning civil engineering, QA/QC, engineering documentation and surveying/geospatial information.

Her work focuses on connecting engineering practice with structured digital information — helping project teams move from fragmented records toward more usable and traceable project and asset information.
