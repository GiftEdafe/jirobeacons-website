Who We Are

An integrated approach to engineering and project information.

Better project outcomes begin with reliable evidence, coordinated delivery and information that remains useful beyond construction.

Jiro-BEACONS Engineering Nigeria Limited brings together engineering, surveying, construction quality and digital information practices to support the delivery and management of physical assets.

Our approach connects surveying and evaluation, quantity and cost considerations, engineering coordination, construction assurance, project documentation and digital asset information.

From existing-building information to construction quality records and digital handover packages, we focus on creating a clearer relationship between what is built, what is verified and what is documented.

Our Philosophy
We don't just record progress. We engineer the information behind it.

Engineering information should do more than fill folders. It should help teams understand what happened, establish what has been verified, trace decisions and make informed choices.

We therefore value evidence, consistency, clear ownership and information structures that support real project requirements.

Our Approach

From site understanding to asset intelligence.

Each project has its own scope and requirements. Our delivery approach connects the stages that help engineering decisions and evidence work together.

01 / ASSESS

Survey & Evaluate

Establish site conditions, spatial context and existing-asset information.

02 / QUANTIFY

Quantity & Cost

Support measurement, quantities, cost planning and commercial visibility.

03 / PLAN

Plan & Coordinate

Bring disciplines, project requirements and delivery information into a coordinated working approach.

04 / BUILD

Build

Support execution with organised information, clear records and coordination.

05 / VERIFY

Verify & Assure

Organise inspection, testing, quality records and supporting evidence for traceability.

06 / ENGINEER

Engineer the Data

Structure project records, drawings, models and asset information into digital packages.

07 / HANDOVER

Digital Handover

Bring deliverables and records together for review and transfer to the receiving team.

08 / INTELLIGENCE

Asset Intelligence

Enable better-informed asset decisions through structured information supporting future operations.
